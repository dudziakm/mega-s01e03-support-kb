How the desk counts

  1. One id, one report: the export repeats reports where its pages overlap.
  2. A report goes under its tenant’s cause (causes.ts): a form report from a booking-v3 tenant that writes the day first is hint; any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. A report the desk read differently (its notes) stays counted where it is filed, with a note under the table.

Intake’s tag is not used: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant.

Why one ticket held them all

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. The same subject lines come in for different problems, so matching on subjects cannot keep these problems apart. Changing that rule needs a new decision record; 0001 stays as accepted.
